What I already understand about Tracey

What can set off a crisis

Silence, broken promises, missed callbacks, cancelled care, abrupt changes, conflicting information, unclear responsibility, reduced support, no closure, privacy breaches, being made to repeat yourself and technology failing when you need it.

How distress can show itself

Strong language, repeated attempts to get an answer, wanting to cancel or leave, a rapid drop in self-worth, feeling rejected or ashamed, numbness or dissociation, and suicidal thoughts becoming louder.

What is more likely to help

Immediate acknowledgement, direct truth, one step at a time, exact dates and named responsibility, no punishment for distress language, privacy, preserving evidence before acting, written follow-through and keeping Mr Gruff included.
